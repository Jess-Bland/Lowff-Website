About LOWFF.

Sport is more than a game.

At LOWFF, we believe every athlete deserves a partner who's with them through every moment of their journey. Whether you're training for your next big competition, pushing through the grind, celebrating a victory, or recovering from a setback, LOWFF is here to support you.

More than a sportswear brand. A community, built by athletes, for athletes.

Every sport, every athlete.

LOWFF makes high-performance sportswear for training, competing and everything in between, from court to gym to track.

Our mission goes beyond a single discipline, we're here for athletes of all kinds, championing the work it takes to reach peak potential.

Built for the highs and the lows.

We know an athlete's journey is far from linear. That's why LOWFF is committed to supporting you through it all, empowering you to become the best athlete you can be, no matter where you are on your journey.

Our products are made to help you rise to every challenge and celebrate every success, and to help you feel part of the community around every session.

Welcome to the LOWFFERS.

The people who move with us. Athletes, training partners, and the moments in between, meet the community.

Home/Delivery & returns

Delivery & returns.

Delivery

Delivery for lowff.co.uk orders is handled by the checkout at time of purchase.

For questions about a specific order, cut-off, carrier, tracking, late or missing parcel, please contact LOWFF with your order number.

Returns policy

Thank you for supporting our small business.

We are committed to providing high-quality products and excellent customer service. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help.

Eligibility for returns

We accept returns within 14 days of receiving your order. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and in the same condition that you received them.

Exchanges

As a small business, we cannot guarantee an exchange for returned items. If you'd like to exchange a product, we suggest initiating a return and placing a new order.

How to return

To initiate a return, please contact LOWFF with your order number and the reason for the return. We will provide instructions for returning your item.

Return shipping

You are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is defective or damaged. Shipping costs are non-refundable.

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, please allow up to 30 days for the refund to be processed back to your original method of payment.
