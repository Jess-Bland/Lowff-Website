Home/About

Sport is more than a game.

At Lowff, we believe that every athlete deserves a partner who's with them through every moment of their journey. Whether you're training for your next big competition, pushing through the grind, celebrating a victory, or recovering from a setback, Lowff is here to support you. We are more than just a sportswear brand — we're a community, built by athletes, for athletes.

Lowff specializes in high-performance sportswear, with a unique focus on racket sports. However, our mission goes beyond a single discipline. We are here for athletes of all kinds, championing their efforts to reach their peak potential.

Built for the highs and the lows

We know that an athlete's journey is far from linear. That's why Lowff is committed to supporting you through it all. Lowff is about empowering you to become the best athlete you can be, no matter where you are in your journey. Our products are made to help you rise to every challenge and celebrate every success.

Home/Delivery & returns

Delivery & returns.

Delivery

Delivery for lowff.co.uk orders is handled by the checkout at time of purchase.

For questions about a specific order — cut-off, carrier, tracking, late or missing parcel — please email lowri@lowff.co.uk with your order number.

Returns policy

Thank you for supporting our small business.

We are committed to providing high-quality products and excellent customer service. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help.

Eligibility for returns

We accept returns within 14 days of receiving your order. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and in the same condition that you received them.

Exchanges

As a small business, we cannot guarantee an exchange for returned items. If you'd like to exchange a product, we suggest initiating a return and placing a new order.

How to return

To initiate a return, please email lowri@lowff.co.uk with your order number and the reason for the return. We will provide instructions for returning your item.

Return shipping

You are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is defective or damaged. Shipping costs are non-refundable.

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, please allow up to 30 days for the refund to be processed back to your original method of payment.
